Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

4Travel Pro (www.4travel.pro) is a free page that links to travel providers such as Kiwi.com, AirHelp, Drimsim, GetTransfer and Localrent. We don't offer accounts, contact forms or newsletters, and we don't ask you for any personal information.

What happens when you visit

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address to keep the service secure and running.
  • Fonts. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with Google.
  • Affiliate tracking. The page uses a script from Travelpayouts, our affiliate network. It can set cookies or similar identifiers so providers know a booking came from 4Travel Pro and we can receive a commission. It does not give us your name, payment details or booking details.
  • Language choice. Your selected language is saved in your browser's local storage so the page opens in the same language next time. It never leaves your device.

When you click a provider

Links take you to the provider's own website. Anything you enter there, such as your name, travel dates or payment details, goes to that provider and is covered by their privacy policy, not this one. Please read it before booking.

Affiliate links

Some links on 4Travel Pro are affiliate links. If you book through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This doesn't change the price you pay.

Your rights

If you are in the EU or UK, you have the right to ask what data is held about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. Since we don't collect personal data ourselves, requests about data held by GitHub, Google, Travelpayouts or a provider are best sent to them directly. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.
